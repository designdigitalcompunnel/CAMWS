Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

CAMWS welcomes higher-education faculty, K–12 teachers, graduate students, independent scholars, and emeriti interested in Greek, Latin, and classical antiquity. Check the membership information for current categories and eligibility.

CAMWS holds an annual Spring Conference at rotating locations and a Southern Section Conference every other year. Check the conference information for current dates, locations, and submission details.

CAMWS publications include the Classical Journal (CJ), Teaching Classical Languages (TCL), the CAMWS Newsletter, and the American Journal of Classical Reception (AJCR). Visit the publications information for current issues and access details.

Visit Grants & Awards for current opportunities, eligibility requirements, deadlines, and application instructions published by CAMWS.
